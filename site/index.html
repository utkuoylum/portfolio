ucm, 2026

An attribution system in Google Tag Manager

Every visit is classified where it starts. Tag Manager reads UTM parameters and click IDs, sorts the visit into channel, channel detail and channel group, and carries that through the lead form, so every lead is attributed before it reaches the CRM. Dashboards and budget decisions run on the same three fields.

Google Tag Manager, UTM taxonomy, click IDs, HubSpot

SEO and GEO, 2026

Roadmaps for visibility in AI answers

It starts with a measured baseline: prompt sets per audience, run repeatedly across ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity and scored for mention and citation share. Then the layers: crawl access through Google and the Bing index, a connected schema.org entity graph, answer-first pages built on the tracked prompts, and the third-party sources models cite. Every month the baseline runs again and priorities are re-ranked from the evidence.

Prompt monitoring, schema.org, llms.txt, hreflang, Search Console API

Zenjob, 2025-2026

n8n workflows that qualify and route leads

Leads arrive through a webhook, get validated, are qualified by an AI agent and routed to Sales, Operations or Marketing. I built the workflows with n8n and Retool, connected to the rest of the stack through REST APIs, OAuth and webhooks.

n8n, Retool, AI agents, REST APIs, OAuth, webhooks

Zenjob, 2025-2026

Lifecycle automation fed by warehouse data

Operational data comes in through Retool and lands in Snowflake. dbt models it into the attributes and events lifecycle messaging needs, and Braze ingests the result to trigger automated journeys, with no manual exports in between.

Retool, Snowflake, dbt, Braze

Segment, mParticle, consent management, RAG
